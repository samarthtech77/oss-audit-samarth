======================================
My Open Source Manifesto
======================================

As a developer who primarily works with Python, I strongly believe in the power of open source. 
Projects like the Linux Kernel have shown how collaboration can lead to robust and scalable systems.

I believe open source breaks down barriers to technology and allows people from different backgrounds 
to contribute and learn together.

By sharing code and ideas freely, we can build better and more inclusive technology for the future.
